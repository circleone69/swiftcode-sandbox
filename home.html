Payment APIs for ecommerce companies

Buckzy is the gateway. Your store keeps the cart and catalog. We give you UPI Intent, Collect, QR, cards, netbanking and wallets behind one Order API and one webhook.

Rails we expose to your checkout

One order object. The hosted checkout (or your own UI via our tokens) collects on these methods.

UPI Intent

Launch GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM with a native intent. Highest conversion on mobile.

UPI Collect

Push a collect request to a VPA. Customer approves in their app.

UPI QR

Dynamic QR per order. Desktop and store-counter flows.

Credit & debit cards

Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, Amex. 3DS2 on the hosted page so PAN never hits you.

Net banking

Redirect to the issuing bank. Status confirmed only on callback + webhook.

Wallets

MobiKwik, Amazon Pay, Paytm wallet as fallback rails.

How an ecommerce integration works

1. Create order

Your backend calls Buckzy with amount, receipt and allowed methods. You get an order_id and a checkout URL.

2. Customer pays

Redirect to Buckzy hosted checkout. Intent / collect / QR / card / NB / wallet all sit on that page.

3. Trust the webhook

payment.captured is the source of truth. Then fulfill the order. The dashboard shows the same ledger.
